Thoughts & Insights

RAG: The Bridge Between Knowledge and Intelligence

Retrieval-Augmented Generation is like having a brilliant research assistant who can instantly access vast libraries of knowledge and synthesize information to answer complex questions. It's not just about storing data—it's about making knowledge accessible and actionable.

Think-On-Graph: Reasoning Like a Doctor

Medical diagnosis isn't linear—it's a web of connected symptoms, conditions, and treatments. By implementing graph-based reasoning, we can mirror how expert physicians think, connecting disparate pieces of information to reach accurate conclusions.

From 200+ LeetCode to Real-World AI

Solving coding problems teaches you to think algorithmically, but building AI systems teaches you to think systematically. Every problem-solving pattern learned on LeetCode becomes a building block for more complex AI architectures.

The Evolution of AI Agents

We've moved from simple chatbots to sophisticated AI agents that can reason, plan, and execute complex workflows. Tools like CrewAI and LangGraph are democratizing multi-agent systems, making collaborative AI accessible to every developer.

Mentoring: Teaching AI to Teach

Having mentored 200+ students, I've learned that the best way to understand a concept is to teach it. This principle applies to AI too—the most robust systems are those that can explain their reasoning and adapt their communication to different audiences.

Vector Databases: The Memory of AI

If transformers are the brain of modern AI, then vector databases are its long-term memory. Tools like FAISS and Chroma don't just store embeddings—they create semantic understanding that enables AI to connect ideas across vast knowledge domains.
